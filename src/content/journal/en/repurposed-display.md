The television is where I want the programme. The question for the deck itself is how little it can show and still be useful from the room.

I am looking at a Motorola Moto X display for that job, set into the front panel. A channel, a playback state, or a single graphic may be enough. A desktop interface shrunk to fit is what I want to avoid.

The screen and the television do different work. One can say what the deck is doing. The other carries the picture.
